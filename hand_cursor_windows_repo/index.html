1. Why Mouth-Click + Pinch-Drag Works Better

Opening your mouth to click means your fingers never twitch while aiming at small buttons, and pinching is 100% dedicated to dragging.

2. Effortless Head-Tilt Scrolling

Tilting your head slightly up or down scrolls smoothly without needing awkward two-finger hand poses. Press N anytime to re-center your neutral head angle.

3. Stabilized Hand Knuckle Anchor

By blending 70% Index Knuckle (MCP) + 30% Index Tip with a fine-aim power curve (1.35), micro-tremors disappear while full-screen reach stays effortless.

Step 1: Save `hand_cursor_mac.py`

Click the green Download `hand_cursor_mac.py` button above (it saves to your Mac's ~/Downloads folder).

Open the macOS Terminal app and paste:

cd ~/Downloads
pip3 install opencv-python mediapipe numpy pyobjc-framework-Quartz pynput
Step 3: Run the App

Test in preview mode first, or run full desktop control:

python3 hand_cursor_mac.py --preview-only
# Or full macOS control:
python3 hand_cursor_mac.py
